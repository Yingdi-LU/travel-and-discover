About Company

At Travel & Discover, we believe that travel is more than visiting new places — it is about discovering new experiences and creating stories worth remembering. We provide personalized travel advice, customized itineraries, and travel companionship for those who want to explore the world in their own way. Whether you are looking for hidden gems, unforgettable adventures, local experiences, or simply someone to make your journey easier and more enjoyable, we are here to help.

About Lydia

Hi, I'm Lydia!

I’ve explored the world in many different ways — trekking through the rainforests of Thailand, watching kangaroos roam across the Australian outback, seeing New York covered in snow, snorkeling in the waters of Vietnam, and kayaking through the spectacular sea caves of Italy.

Along the way, I’ve learned that the best travel experiences are not always found in guidebooks. I created Travel and Discover to share that feeling with others and help you create journeys that are truly your own.
